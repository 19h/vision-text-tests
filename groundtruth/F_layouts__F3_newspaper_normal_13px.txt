--- COLUMN 1: NORTH GATE ---
The inspection began at the north gate,
where two auditors compared the printed
manifest against the physical count.
Discrepancies were rare but
consequential: a single mislabelled pallet
could hold up an entire convoy for eleven
hours. The senior auditor, working from a
clipboard, recorded each variance in a
ruled notebook and initialled the margin.

transcribed into the ledger, cross-checked
against the weighbridge tape, and filed.
